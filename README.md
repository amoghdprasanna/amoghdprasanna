I'm a graduate researcher at the University of Maryland, working with Prof. Shabnam Jabeen and Dr. Tanay Roy (Fermilab SQMS). I care about the gap between the circuit we write and what the hardware actually does.

<br>

**Now**

Learning to compile arbitrary SU(*d*) unitaries into native gate sequences on superconducting transmon qudits, with training objectives that account for leakage and anharmonicity.

Training quantum circuit Born machines on hadron momentum distributions from Pythia 8, and keeping what they learn intact under IBM hardware noise.

Building an open-source decoding toolkit for simulating and decoding topological codes.
